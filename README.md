# infosys-springboard-7-cybersecurityAnalysis-TeamA
Cybersecurity Threat Analysis Using Power BI – Infosys Springboard Virtual Internship 7.0, Team A.
Cyber Threat Analytics Dashboard — Milestone 2 Task Division
Project Name

Cyber Threat Analytics Platform with Incident Visualization System

Milestone: Milestone 2 — Major Data Analysis
Tool: Microsoft Power BI
Dataset: Cybersecurity Attacks Dataset
Theme: Cybersecurity Dark Neon

Page 1 — Cyber Threat Intelligence Overview

Objective: Analyze the overall incident volume, threat severity, attack categories, and historical incident trends.

1. Overall Incident Analysis
Display the total number of cybersecurity incidents using KPI cards.
Display high-severity incident counts.
Display high-risk incident counts.
Calculate and validate the total incident count against the preprocessed dataset.
Ensure KPI values respond correctly to interactive filters.

Recommended visuals:

Total Incidents — KPI card.
High-Severity Incidents — KPI card.
High-Risk Incidents — KPI card.
Average Anomaly Score — KPI card.

Objective: Provide a quick overview of the incident volume and recorded threat situation.

2. Threat Severity Analysis
Create a donut chart showing the distribution of incidents by severity level.
Display the incident count and percentage for each severity category.
Use consistent colors to distinguish severity levels.
Validate the percentages against the total number of incidents.

Recommended visual: Donut chart.

Objective: Understand the distribution of incident severity and identify the proportion of high-severity records.

3. Threat Category Analysis
Create a horizontal bar chart showing incident counts by attack type.
Sort attack categories in descending order.
Highlight the top five attack categories, where applicable.
Use clear labels and data values.
Configure interactions with other relevant charts.

Recommended visual: Horizontal bar chart.

Objective: Identify the most frequently recorded attack categories and understand their contribution to the incident dataset.

4. Time-Based Incident Trend Analysis
Create a line chart showing incident counts over time.
Use the Incident Date field for chronological analysis.
Aggregate incidents by month when the date range supports monthly analysis.
Arrange dates in chronological order.
Identify periods with higher or lower recorded incident volumes.

Recommended visual: Line chart.

Objective: Analyze historical incident patterns and identify periods that may require further investigation.

Page 2 — Threat Risk and Network Intelligence

Objective: Analyze risk classifications, anomaly scores, network protocols, and geographical incident distribution.

5. Threat Risk Analysis
Display the distribution of incidents by Risk Category.
Compare High Risk, Medium Risk, and Low Risk records.
Display the average anomaly score using a KPI card.
Validate risk classifications against the project's predefined thresholds.
Use consistent colors for each risk category.

Recommended visuals:

Risk Category Distribution — Column chart.
Average Anomaly Score — KPI card.

Objective: Understand the distribution of project-defined risk categories and compare anomaly scores across the incident dataset.

Risk classification rules:

High Risk: Anomaly Score ≥ 70.
Medium Risk: Anomaly Score ≥ 40 and < 70.
Low Risk: Anomaly Score < 40.

Note: These thresholds are specific to this project and are not universal cybersecurity risk standards.

6. Network Protocol Analysis
Create a chart showing the distribution of network protocols.
Compare protocols such as TCP, UDP, and ICMP where present.
Display incident counts and percentages.
Compare average packet length across protocols, if the data supports the analysis.
Use clear titles, labels, and tooltips.

Recommended visuals:

Network Protocol Distribution — Donut chart.
Average Packet Length by Protocol — Column chart.

Objective: Understand the distribution of network protocols and compare recorded packet characteristics.

7. Geographical Threat Analysis
Analyze incident records using the available geographical fields.
Compare incident counts across Region/Country or Location.
Identify geographical categories with the highest recorded incident counts.
Use a map only if the location data is sufficiently accurate and suitable.
Use a ranked horizontal bar chart as an alternative when a map is unsuitable.

Recommended visuals:

Incidents by Region/Country — Horizontal bar chart.
Top Locations by Incident Count — Bar chart or table.

Objective: Explore the geographical distribution of recorded incidents and identify locations with higher reported incident volumes.

Note: Geographical conclusions must reflect what the dataset's location fields actually represent.

Design Guidelines
Use a consistent dark background across both pages.
Place KPI cards near the top of each page.
Use neon accents selectively to emphasize important information.
Maintain readable chart labels and titles.
Use consistent colors for severity and risk categories.
Avoid excessive colors and unnecessary visual clutter.

A professional, interactive Power BI report containing two Milestone 2 pages.

The report will provide an overview of incident volume, threat severity, attack categories, historical trends, risk classifications, network protocols, and geographical distribution where supported by the dataset.
